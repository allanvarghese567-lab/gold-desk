Market structure · BOS · CHoCH

Green = up · Red = down · Yellow RANGE = sideways (warning). BOS = trend may continue. CHoCH (blinks) = trend may change. Only a candle close beyond the level counts — not a wick alone.

CRT · TBS · Candle Range Theory

CRT = one candle’s high/low is the range; next bar sweeps one side and closes back inside — bias toward the other side. TBS (gold, blinks) = Turtle Body Soup: false break, body closes back. Yellow RANGE = sideways warning. Lines = CRT high, low, 50%.
